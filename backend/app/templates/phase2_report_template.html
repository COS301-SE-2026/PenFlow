Assessment Limitations

This assessment represents externally observable conditions at the time the scan was performed.

  • The assessment evaluates externally reachable systems and does not perform authenticated inspections of internal application state.
  • Software versions may not be identifiable when services suppress or obscure version information.
  • Reverse proxies, CDNs, and security gateways may expose edge infrastructure rather than the underlying origin environment.
  • Vulnerability correlation depends on reliable product and version identification.
  • The absence of a reported finding does not guarentee that a system is free of security weaknesses.

PHASE 3

Go Beyond Automated Assessment

Automated scanning can identify exposed services, configuration weaknesses, known vulnerabilities, and observable attack-surface changes. Some security risks require human intervention to determine practical exploitability and real-world impact.

Validate Findings Confirm whether identified weaknesses can be exploited in practice and determine their actual impact.
Investigate Attack Paths Assess whether individual exposures can be combined into meaninful attack chains.
Human-Led Testing Examine behaviours, application logic, and security controls beyond the reach of automated scanning.
Need deeper assurance? Proceed to a controlled penetration test to investigate the target beyond the scope of automated external assessment.
